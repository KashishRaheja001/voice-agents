# Compliance (India)

Not legal advice. Sources below are mostly commentary and vendor blogs; confirm against
the TRAI Gazette text before calling real leads.

## What we know so far

- TRAI's Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference (Third Amendment)
  Regulations, 2026 reportedly require anyone placing automated or pre-recorded calls
  to declare their calling number series to telecom providers and record it on DLT.
- Undeclared automated traffic is treated as unsolicited communication.
- Promotional calls use the 140 series; service and transactional calls are moving to a
  separate series (160 / 1600). Exact numbering rules need checking.
- Consent must be recorded; TRAI is moving consent onto DLT. Offer opt-out on every
  call.
- Per-call penalty figures in vendor blogs (₹1,000–10,000) are unverified.

## Our operating rules

1. Testing: call only ourselves and people who agreed.
2. First use case: call back people who submitted an enquiry (they gave consent).
   No cold calling.
3. Before going live with a customer, settle in writing:
   - Who holds DLT registration: the customer (principal entity) or us?
   - Does Vobiz support declared number series and DLT for AI calls? Get it in writing.
   - The customer confirms the leads gave consent.
4. "Not interested" or "don't call" ends the call and marks the number do-not-call.
